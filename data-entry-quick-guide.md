# Data Entry Quick Guide: Immunization Registration Records

**For:** data entry staff entering child registration records from register photos
**Goal:** every record is entered exactly as written in the register, so it passes review the first time.

---

## Before you enter a record

1. Open the register photo and check that you can read every line.
2. If the photo is blurry, cropped, has glare or is missing, **do not guess**. Flag the record to the reviewer.
3. Check you are not entering a child who is already in the system (same name, date of birth and phone number).

## Entering each field

| Field | How to enter it | Mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Child name | Copy the spelling in the register exactly. | Correcting a spelling you think is wrong. |
| Date of birth | Day, month, year as written. | Swapping day and month (04/10 is not 10/04). |
| Sex | F or M. | Leaving it blank. |
| Caregiver phone | 11 digits, starting 07, 08 or 09. No spaces, dashes or +234. | The letter **O** instead of the number **0**. Dropping the first 0. |
| Vaccine received | Choose from the list. | Typing a free-text name. |
| Vaccine date | Date written in the register. | A date before the child was born, or in the future. |
| Next appointment | Date written in the register. | A date on or before the vaccine date. |

## Before you submit: 5-point check

- [ ] Name matches the register letter for letter.
- [ ] Phone number has 11 digits and no letter O.
- [ ] Vaccine date is after the date of birth and not in the future.
- [ ] Next appointment is after the vaccine date.
- [ ] No field left blank without a note to the reviewer.

## What happens after you submit

- A reviewer grades each record: **A** goes forward, **B** comes back to you to fix, **C** is discarded.
- A sample of records is entered twice by different people. Where the two entries differ, the reviewer checks the photo and decides which is right.
- You will get feedback listing the record IDs that need attention and why. Fix them the same day if you can.

## When you are unsure

Do not guess. Flag the record, write what you could not read, and ask the reviewer. A flagged record costs a minute. A wrong phone number means a caregiver never gets the reminder.
